0.  DESIGN THESIS & NON-NEGOTIABLES
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

0.1  THE THESIS

  This is not a data-exploration tool. It is an instrument that helps a
  police officer decide whether to send a legal demand to a company, and
  helps a court decide whether to believe the result.

  Every design decision follows from that. A dashboard that looks impressive
  but lets an officer sign a 42%-confidence attribution without noticing is
  a failed design regardless of its visual quality.

0.2  THE FIVE NON-NEGOTIABLES

  N1  UNCERTAINTY IS NEVER DECORATIVE.
      Confidence is shown as a distribution with a stated meaning, never as
      a bare number, never as a green tick, never as a word like "high".

  N2  THE CONSEQUENTIAL ACTION IS ALWAYS THE HARDEST ONE TO REACH.
      Viewing is one click. Signing and dispatching is a deliberate,
      multi-step, re-authenticated flow with a named recipient.

  N3  NOTHING IS HIDDEN THAT AFFECTS THE EVIDENCE.
      Pruned edges, conflicted sources, failed proofs, single-source data,
      and human overrides are surfaced on the artefact itself — never only
      in a log or a collapsed panel that defaults closed.

  N4  THE GRAPH IS NEVER THE ONLY REPRESENTATION.
      Every graph has an equivalent table. Some officers think in tables,
      screen readers require tables, and courts print tables.

  N5  THE SYSTEM NEVER SOUNDS CERTAIN WHEN IT ISN'T.
      Microcopy is bound by §25. "Identified" and "attributed" are reserved
      words with defined thresholds. "Suspect" is never applied to a person.

0.3  DESIGN PRINCIPLES

  DP1  CONSEQUENCE-PROPORTIONAL FRICTION
       UI friction scales with legal consequence, not with engineering cost.

  DP2  PROGRESSIVE TECHNICAL DISCLOSURE
       Layer 1: plain language for the IO.
       Layer 2: structured detail for the analyst.
       Layer 3: raw JSON / hashes / proofs for the expert and the court.
       Layer 1 is the default. Layer 3 is always reachable in ≤2 clicks.

  DP3  THE SCREEN EXPLAINS ITSELF
       Any officer, mid-case, at 2am, should be able to answer "what am I
       looking at and what should I do next" from the screen alone.

  DP4  NO DARK PATTERNS, INCLUDING WELL-INTENTIONED ONES
       Never nudge toward dispatch. Never pre-tick a consent. Never make
       "cancel" visually secondary on a destructive dialog.

  DP5  DENSITY WITH BREATHING ROOM
       Forensic work is dense. But the primary decision on each screen gets
       whitespace around it so it cannot be missed in the density.

  DP6  EVERY NUMBER IS CLICKABLE TO ITS SOURCE
       If the UI shows a figure, clicking it reveals where it came from.

1.  USER CONTEXT & ENVIRONMENTAL CONSTRAINTS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.1  THE REAL DEPLOYMENT ENVIRONMENT (design for this, not for a demo laptop)

  Display        : 1366×768 is the design floor. 1920×1080 is the common case.
                   4K is not assumed anywhere.
  GPU            : Integrated graphics. The graph canvas must work without a
                   discrete GPU.
  Network        : Government network, often 2–10 Mbps shared, with latency
                   spikes. Assume intermittent, not broken.
  Browser        : Chromium-based, sometimes a version or two behind. Design
                   to widely-supported CSS; no bleeding-edge-only features on
                   the critical path.
  Lighting       : Fluorescent office lighting, and night-shift work. Both a
                   dark and a light theme are required — dark for extended
                   night sessions, light because projectors and printouts in
                   court settings need it.
  Interruption   : The officer will be interrupted constantly. Every flow
                   must be resumable; nothing is lost by navigating away.
  Skill          : See PRD personas. Assume no blockchain literacy for the
                   primary persona.
  Stress         : The 24–72h dispersal window creates time pressure. Under
                   time pressure people click the biggest button. Design
                   accordingly: the biggest button must never be the riskiest.

1.2  WHAT THIS MEANS CONCRETELY

  - Autosave everything. No modal that loses work on accidental dismissal.
  - Every long operation is backgroundable with a notification on completion.
  - No feature that requires a second monitor.
  - No hover-only affordances (touch laptops exist; and hover is invisible).
  - Print stylesheets are a first-class requirement, not an afterthought.

3.  NAVIGATION MODEL
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1  GLOBAL SHELL

  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ [PHANTASM]  Cases  Review(3)  Approvals(1)  Admin   [MOCK] [?] [SI R] │ ← 56px
  ├───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │                                                                       │
  │                          ROUTE CONTENT                                │
  │                                                                       │
  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  - Header is fixed, 56px, never scrolls away.
  - Badge counts on Review/Approvals are live and are the primary work driver.
  - [MOCK] environment chip is ALWAYS visible when
    disclosure_requests.is_mock_environment is true or
    system_config['sahyog.environment'] = "MOCK". Amber, uppercase,
    non-dismissible. This is a hard requirement from PRD US-SHY-011 AC5 —
    a demo must never be mistakable for a live government integration.
  - Role chip next to the user name so the user always knows their capability.

3.2  CASE-SCOPED SUB-NAVIGATION

  Inside a case, a persistent context bar sits under the global header:

  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ ← PHM-2026-TN-004182 · Investment fraud · ₹18,00,000 · ACTIVE          │
  │   Overview | Traces (2) | Dossiers (1) | Requests (0) | Files | Audit  │
  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  The case reference, type, loss amount and status are ALWAYS on screen
  inside a case. An officer working six cases must never have to check which
  one they are in before acting.

3.3  KEYBOARD NAVIGATION
  g then d    → dashboard          /  → focus search
  g then c    → cases              ?  → keyboard shortcut overlay
  g then r    → review queue       Esc → close drawer/modal (never submits)
  g then a    → approvals
  Ctrl/Cmd+K  → command palette (navigate, open case by reference, launch)

3.4  BREADCRUMBS
  Shown on every page below case level. Every segment is a real link.

  └──────────────────┴─────────────┴──────────────┴────────────────────────┘

4.3  SPACING SCALE (4px base)
  --space-1  4px   --space-2  8px   --space-3 12px   --space-4 16px
  --space-5 20px   --space-6 24px   --space-8 32px   --space-10 40px
  --space-12 48px  --space-16 64px

4.4  RADII & ELEVATION
  --radius-sm 4px  --radius-md 6px  --radius-lg 10px  --radius-full 999px
  --elev-1  0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.4)        cards
  --elev-2  0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.5)       drawers
  --elev-3  0 12px 32px rgba(0,0,0,.6)      modals
  Elevation is subtle. This is a forensic tool, not a consumer app; heavy
  shadows read as decoration and cost rendering performance on the canvas.

4.5  LIGHT THEME
  Required, not optional. Same token names, remapped:
    --surface-base #FFFFFF, --surface-raised #F6F8FA, --text-primary #0B0E13.
  Classification hues shift to their 600-weight variants for contrast on
  white. All contrast ratios re-verified independently for the light theme —
  a palette that passes in dark does not automatically pass in light.

5.  DESIGN SYSTEM — TYPOGRAPHY
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  FAMILIES
    UI text        : Inter (fallback: system-ui, Segoe UI, Roboto)
    Monospace      : JetBrains Mono (fallback: ui-monospace, Consolas)
    Indic scripts  : Noto Sans Devanagari / Noto Sans Tamil for §30

  MONOSPACE IS MANDATORY FOR: addresses, transaction hashes, block hashes,
  Merkle proof hashes, case references, request references. These are
  compared character-by-character by humans; a proportional font makes
  0/O and l/1/I confusions likely and a transposition error here can send a
  legal demand to the wrong company.

  SCALE
    display   28px / 34px / 600    page titles
    h1        22px / 28px / 600    section titles
    h2        18px / 24px / 600    card titles
    h3        15px / 20px / 600    subsection
    body      14px / 21px / 400    default
    body-sm   13px / 19px / 400    dense tables
    caption   12px / 16px / 400    labels, metadata
    micro     11px / 14px / 500    badges, chips (uppercase, +0.04em)
    mono      13px / 20px / 400    hashes and addresses
    mono-sm   12px / 18px / 400    dense hash tables

  14px body is the floor. Do not go below it for readable content; officers
  work long shifts and 12px body text on a 1366px laptop is a fatigue source.

  ADDRESS TRUNCATION RULE
    Display as first 8 … last 6 characters, e.g. bc1qxy2k…8mzk9v.
    NEVER truncate in the middle of an evidentiary export.
    Full value always available via click-to-copy and via the table view.
    A truncated address is always accompanied by a copy affordance —
    an officer retyping a truncated address from screen is a defect vector.

6.  DESIGN SYSTEM — COMPONENT LIBRARY
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

6.1  BUTTONS
  Variants : primary / secondary / ghost / danger / danger-confirm
  Sizes    : sm 28px, md 34px, lg 40px
  Rules    :
    - One primary button per view. If two things look equally primary, the
      design has failed to decide what the user should do.
    - Destructive and legally-consequential actions use `danger-confirm`,
      which requires the user to type a confirmation token (see §6.9).
    - Disabled buttons ALWAYS carry a tooltip stating why they are disabled.
      A silently disabled Dispatch button is a support ticket.
    - Loading state replaces the label with a spinner + the verb in progress
      ("Tracing…"), never an unlabelled spinner.

6.2  CONFIDENCE METER  (the single most important component in the product)

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  ATTRIBUTION CONFIDENCE                                             │
  │                                                                     │
  │   0%        25%        50%        75%       100%                    │
  │   ├──────────┼──────────┼──────────┼─────────┤                      │
  │   ░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░▓▓▓▓▓███▓▓▓░░░░░                       │
  │                            ╰─ 87.1% ── 91.3% ── 94.0% ─╯            │
  │                               CI low   point    CI high             │
  │                    ┊                                                │
  │                    ┊ action threshold 75%                           │
  │                                                                     │
  │  91.3%  [ ABOVE ACTION THRESHOLD ]                                  │
  │                                                                     │
  │  This is the estimated probability that the traced funds reached    │
  │  the identified custodial service. It is NOT a probability that     │
  │  any person committed an offence.                                   │
  │                                                                     │
  │  Based on 10,000 simulated traversals · 95% credible interval       │
  │  [ How was this calculated? ]                                       │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  REQUIREMENTS:
    - The distribution shape is always drawn, not just the point value.
    - The action threshold marker is always drawn, even when far away.
    - The disclaimer sentence is fixed text, not editable, not collapsible.
      It exists because a confidence number without a stated referent is the
      most dangerous artefact this product can produce.
    - Sample count and CI level always shown.
    - "How was this calculated?" opens the confidence explainer (§15).
    - When mc_converged = false, the meter renders in a striped pattern with
      the label "Estimate did not converge — treat as indicative only".

6.3  NODE CHIP
  Compact representation of an address used in tables, drawers and lists.

    [⬡ VASP]  bc1qxy2k…8mzk9v  ·  WazirX (deposit)  ·  92%   [⧉]

  Elements: shape+colour class icon, mono truncated address, entity name if
  known, classification probability, copy button. Clicking opens §13.

6.4  EVIDENCE BADGE ROW
  Renders on every hop and every cited transaction:

    [✓ Proof verified] [✓✓ 2 sources agree] [⛓ 143 confirmations]

  Failure variants, which are never suppressed:
    [✕ Proof failed] [⚠ Sources disagree] [◷ Below finality threshold]
    [◐ Single source] [✎ Human override] [~ Filtered edge]

6.5  DATA TABLE
  - Sticky header, sticky first column on horizontal scroll.
  - Column visibility control; user preference persisted per table.
  - Sort, filter, and full-text within-table search.
  - Row density toggle: comfortable (default) / compact.
  - Export to CSV always present — an officer will need the data in Excel,
    and pretending otherwise means they screenshot it instead.
  - Selection with a bulk-action bar that appears only when rows are selected.
  - Virtualised rendering above 100 rows.

6.6  TIMELINE
  Vertical, newest-first by default with a toggle. Each entry: actor avatar
  or system glyph, action verb, relative time with absolute on hover, and an
  expandable detail payload rendered as formatted JSON at Layer 3.

6.7  BANNER / CALLOUT
  Variants: info / warning / danger / mock / legal.
  The `legal` variant has a distinct treatment (left rule + serif label) and
  is used for statutory notices and the limitations disclosure. It cannot be
  dismissed. Its content is not user-editable.

6.8  EMPTY STATE
  Every empty state has: an illustration or glyph, one sentence of what this
  area is for, and exactly one primary action. Never a bare "No data".

6.9  CONFIRMATION DIALOG — TIERED

  TIER 1 (reversible) — simple confirm/cancel.
  TIER 2 (significant) — confirm with a summary of consequences listed.
  TIER 3 (legally consequential, e.g. dispatch to a VASP):
    ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
    │  Dispatch disclosure request                                      │
    │                                                                   │
    │  You are about to serve a legal request on:                       │
    │      WazirX (Zanmai Labs Pvt Ltd)                                 │
    │      Nodal officer: <name>, <designation>                         │
    │                                                                   │
    │  Legal basis     Section 94, BNSS 2023                            │
    │  Confidence      91.3%  (95% CI 87.1–94.0)                        │
    │  Deposit address bc1qxy2k…8mzk9v                                  │
    │  Transactions    4                                                │
    │  Time window     12 Feb 2026 – 14 Feb 2026                        │
    │                                                                   │
    │  This action is recorded in the audit log and cannot be undone.   │
    │  A withdrawal notice would have to be issued separately.          │
    │                                                                   │
    │  Type the exchange name to confirm:  [________________]           │
    │                                                                   │
    │                       [ Cancel ]    [ Sign and dispatch ]         │
    └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Typing the recipient name is deliberate friction: it is the specific error
  this product must never make (PRD RSK-001), and forcing the officer to
  read and reproduce the recipient's name is the cheapest effective guard.

6.10  COMMAND PALETTE (Ctrl/Cmd+K)
  Navigate, open case by reference, search address within accessible cases,
  jump to a trace. Does NOT expose actions the user lacks permission for —
  absent, not disabled, so the palette does not enumerate capabilities.

6.11  COPY-TO-CLIPBOARD
  Every hash, address, and reference. Feedback is a 1.2s inline "Copied"
  chip, never a floating toast that the user may miss.

7.  SCREEN SPEC 01 — LOGIN & SESSION
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  LAYOUT: centred card, 400px, on a plain surface. No marketing imagery.

  ELEMENTS
    - Agency SSO button (primary): "Sign in with department account"
    - Local credentials (secondary, non-production environments only)
    - MFA step as a separate screen, never inline with the password
    - Environment chip if non-production: [ DEVELOPMENT ] / [ MOCK SAHYOG ]
    - Legal notice: authorised use only, activity is monitored, misuse is an
      offence. Present on the login screen itself — it is where a warning
      about authorised access has to be.

  BEHAVIOUR
    - No enumeration: a wrong username and a wrong password produce identical
      messaging and identical timing.
    - Lockout after configured failures with a clear duration, never silent.
    - Session expiry shows a warning at T-2 minutes with an extend action.
    - Re-authentication for privileged actions is a modal that preserves all
      page state — the officer never loses a half-composed request to it.

    Non-path nodes render at 35% opacity by default. The officer's eye should

12.3  INTERACTIONS

  click node          → open inspector drawer (§13)
  double-click node   → expand one hop from that node (budget-checked)
  click edge          → transaction detail popover with proof status
  hover               → highlight the connected subpath
  drag                → pan;  scroll → zoom;  Ctrl+scroll → zoom fine
  right-click node    → context menu: copy address, open in explorer,
                        reclassify (role-gated), exclude from path,
                        mark as VASP-confirmed (role-gated)
  box-select          → select multiple, then bulk-inspect in table view
  keyboard            → Tab traverses nodes in path order; Enter opens the
                        inspector; arrow keys move along the path. This is
                        not optional (§26).

12.4  LAYOUT MODES
   - HIERARCHICAL (default): seed at left, hops advancing rightward. This is
     the layout that maps to how the officer thinks: money moved this way.
   - FORCE-DIRECTED: for exploring dense neighbourhoods.
   - TEMPORAL: x-axis is time, revealing burst structure directly. This is
     the layout where a peel chain becomes visually obvious.
   Layout choice is persisted per user.

12.5  TIMELINE SCRUBBER (optional layer)
   A horizontal control below the canvas that filters edges by block time,
   letting the officer replay the dispersal. Playback is manual by default;
   auto-play is opt-in and pausable.

12.6  PERFORMANCE
   - WebGL rendering (Cytoscape.js WebGL or equivalent).
   - ≥30 FPS at 5,000 nodes / 15,000 edges on integrated graphics.
   - Above that: progressive disclosure — render the path plus a 2-hop
     neighbourhood, with an explicit "N further nodes not shown" control.
     Never silently drop nodes; a graph that quietly omits data is worse
     than a graph that says it is omitting data.
   - Level-of-detail: labels hide below a zoom threshold; node detail
     simplifies at distance.

12.7  MANDATORY TABLE VIEW TOGGLE
   A single control switches the entire canvas to §14's table representation
   of exactly the same data. Not a reduced version — the same data. This
   satisfies N4, NFR-ACC-003, and the reality that some officers simply work
   faster in tables.

  VERSIONING
    Regeneration requires a stated reason and produces v(n+1) linked to the
    prior version. The superseded version remains downloadable, watermarked
    SUPERSEDED, because destroying a superseded evidentiary artefact is
    itself an evidential problem.

  INVALIDATION
    If a re-validation job detects a reorg affecting a cited transaction, the
    dossier is marked INVALIDATED with a red banner on the case, on the
    dossier list, and stamped on the file itself on any subsequent download.

19.  SCREEN SPEC 13 — REVIEW QUEUE (ANALYST)
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  PURPOSE: let an analyst triage 40 machine attributions a day.

  LAYOUT: list left (40%), detail right (60%), keyboard-driven.

  LIST ROW
    Case ref · confidence meter · terminal entity · hop count · flags
    Flags: [mixer] [bridge] [conflicted] [single-source] [proof-failed]
           [offshore] [zero-day-wallet]

  SORTING DEFAULT
    By "review value": low confidence AND high traced value first. An
    analyst's scarce attention should go where the machine is least sure and
    the money is largest — not simply to the newest item.

  DETAIL PANE
    Condensed graph, hop table, confidence decomposition, and the three
    analyst actions:
      [ Accept attribution ]   → advances the case
      [ Reclassify a node ]    → §13 override flow
      [ Reject attribution ]   → requires a written reason; the case returns
                                 to the IO with the analyst's note
    Every action requires the analyst to have opened the evidence tab at
    least once for that trace. Accepting without looking is possible in a
    bad tool; here the button stays disabled with the tooltip "Open the
    evidence tab before accepting."

  KEYBOARD
    j/k next/previous · a accept · r reject · o open full trace · ? shortcuts

24.  STATE MATRIX — EMPTY / LOADING / ERROR / PARTIAL
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Every data surface implements all five states. "It works when data exists"
  is half a feature.

  ┌──────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │ State            │ Requirement                                           │
  ├──────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
  │ EMPTY            │ Explain the area's purpose + one primary action.       │
  │                  │ Never "No data available."                            │
  │ LOADING          │ Skeleton matching the final layout, not a spinner on   │
  │                  │ a blank page. Long ops show real progress (§11.2).     │
  │ ERROR            │ What failed, whether the user's work is safe, what to  │
  │                  │ do next, a retry action, and a support reference id.   │
  │                  │ Never a raw stack trace or an HTTP status alone.       │
  │ PARTIAL          │ Show what succeeded, name what did not, and say how    │
  │                  │ that affects conclusions. e.g. "Blockchair did not     │
  │                  │ respond. 3 transactions are single-source and are      │
  │                  │ excluded from the primary evidence path."              │
  │ STALE            │ When displayed data may be outdated (cached, or a      │
  │                  │ model has since been promoted), a timestamp and a      │
  │                  │ refresh action are shown.                              │
  └──────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  PARTIAL IS THE MOST IMPORTANT STATE IN THIS PRODUCT.
  Multi-source ingestion means partial success is the normal case, not an
  edge case. A UI that renders partial results as if they were complete is
  the single most likely way this system produces a wrong legal outcome.


═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
25.  MICROCOPY STANDARDS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

25.1  RESERVED WORDS — defined meanings, used only as defined

  IDENTIFIED     Confidence ≥ action threshold AND terminal entity matched
                 in the registry. Nothing else may use this word.
  ATTRIBUTED     A path was found and scored, regardless of threshold.
  CANDIDATE      Below threshold; requires review.
  SUSPECT        Applies to an ADDRESS only. Never to a person. There is no
                 screen in this product that calls a human a suspect.
  CONFIRMED      Only when a VASP has responded confirming the address.
  VERIFIED       Only for cryptographic proof verification.
  LIKELY / PROBABLE — banned. Use the number.

25.2  BANNED PHRASINGS
  ✗ "Wallet owner identified"        → we identify services, not owners
  ✗ "100% confidence"                → the meter caps display at 99.9%
  ✗ "Criminal wallet"                → "address associated with the reported
                                        incident"
  ✗ "Proven"                         → "verified" for proofs; nothing else
  ✗ "AI determined"                  → name the model and its version
  ✗ "Trace complete" when a budget   → "Trace stopped: time budget reached"
     terminated the search

25.3  TONE
  Plain, precise, calm. No exclamation marks anywhere in the product. No
  congratulatory language on completing a trace — a completed trace is a
  step in an investigation into someone's financial loss, and the interface
  should not celebrate it.

25.4  ERROR MESSAGE TEMPLATE
    [What happened]. [Whether your work is safe]. [What to do next].
    Example:
      "The Blockchair data source did not respond. Your trace is saved and
       3 transactions were verified against Etherscan only. You can retry
       cross-validation, or continue with those transactions marked as
       single-source."

25.5  TOOLTIP GLOSSARY (NFR-USE-002)
  Every technical term has an inline definition on hover AND on focus:
    UTXO, peel chain, mixing service, bridge, deposit address, hot wallet,
    confirmation depth, Merkle proof, absorption state, credible interval,
    branching ratio, cross-validation, abstention.
  Definitions are written for the primary persona, not for an engineer.
  Example — Merkle proof: "A short cryptographic receipt that proves this
  transaction really was included in this block. Anyone can check it without
  trusting PHANTASM."

27.  MOTION & FEEDBACK
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  DURATIONS   micro 120ms · standard 200ms · complex 320ms
  EASING      standard cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1); decelerate for entrances
  RULES
    - Motion communicates causality (this drawer came from that node) or
      state change. Never decoration.
    - No animation on the graph canvas during interaction; it competes with
      the user's own panning and costs frames.
    - Trace stage transitions animate once, subtly.
    - Loading skeletons pulse at low amplitude; a high-contrast shimmer is
      distracting over a long trace.
    - prefers-reduced-motion is honoured completely (A11Y-08).
  HAPTICS/SOUND
    None. This is an office tool used in shared rooms.

31.  UI SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  UISEC-01  All external strings (token names, contract metadata, provider
            fields, VASP names) are escaped and length-bounded on render.
            Token metadata is attacker-controlled data (PRD SR-008) — a
            token named with a script payload is a real, cheap attack.
  UISEC-02  No dangerouslySetInnerHTML anywhere. Lint-enforced.
  UISEC-03  Strict CSP: no inline scripts, no eval, explicit connect-src.
  UISEC-04  Permission checks are enforced server-side; the UI hides
            unauthorised actions as a usability measure and never as the
            security control.
  UISEC-05  Session expiry warning; automatic lock on idle with all state
            preserved behind the lock screen.
  UISEC-06  Screenshots of case data are outside the product's control, but
            the UI displays a per-session watermark (user id + timestamp) on
            evidence-bearing views to deter uncontrolled redistribution.
  UISEC-07  Clipboard writes of full addresses/hashes are audited events.
  UISEC-08  Signed URLs for evidence objects expire in ≤5 minutes and are
            never placed in the browser history or in an href that could be
            copied and shared.
  UISEC-09  The MOCK environment chip cannot be hidden by CSS override,
            user preference, or a URL parameter.

32.  USABILITY VALIDATION PLAN
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  UV-01  MODERATED TASK TEST, ≥5 participants matching the primary persona
         (no blockchain background), after ≤2 hours of training.
         Task: complete Journey A end-to-end unaided.
         Pass criterion: ≥4 of 5 complete without assistance, and none
         dispatch a below-threshold attribution.

  UV-02  THE CRITICAL-ERROR TEST. Present a 43%-confidence attribution and
         observe whether the participant notices, and what they do. Any
         participant who attempts to dispatch it without comment is a
         design failure, not a training failure.

  UV-03  THE MIXER TEST (Journey B). Present a trace that terminates at a
         mixer. Success is the participant correctly describing what the
         system found and what it did not, in their own words.

  UV-04  SUPERVISOR TEST. A legally-literate non-specialist reviews a
         sign-off screen for 5 minutes and is then asked what could be wrong
         with the attribution. Success is them naming at least two real
         limitations from §20's "What could be wrong" section.

  UV-05  GREYSCALE REVIEW at every design sign-off (A11Y-03).

  UV-06  PRINT REVIEW: print the hop table and the dossier; confirm both are
         usable in a physical case file with no on-screen context.

  UV-07  SLOW-NETWORK TEST at 2 Mbps with 300ms latency; confirm no screen
         becomes unusable and every long operation shows honest progress.
